# Superstore Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end business intelligence analysis built in Looker Studio to evaluate sales, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance and geographic trends using transaction-level retail data.

## Problem Statement

The business generates revenue across multiple products, customer segments and geographic regions, but headline sales figures alone do not explain whether that growth is profitable.

The objective was to build an interactive dashboard that helps decision-makers understand:

- Which categories and products drive revenue and profit
- Which customer segments contribute the most value
- Where margins are being lost
- Which regions and states perform best
- How performance changes over time

## Key Findings

- Sales increased by approximately 15.8%.
- Profit increased by approximately 27.8%.
- Technology was the strongest revenue category.
- The West region delivered strong sales and profitability.
- Some high-sales products generated weak or negative profit.
- Consumer customers contributed the largest share of revenue.

## Recommendations

- Protect profitable growth in strong categories and regions.
- Review loss-making products and discount strategies.
- Analyse high-performing regions and replicate successful practices.
- Strengthen customer retention and cross-selling.
- Use both sales and profit margin when evaluating expansion opportunities.
